Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

What we collect

Highlight Corner collects nothing directly. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, and no analytics of our own. Scores, stats, and fantasy data are fetched live in your browser from public sports data providers (ESPN and Sleeper); we never see or store those requests.

Stored on your device only

Your theme choice, spoiler-free preference, favorite teams, and watched games are saved in your browser's local storage, on your device only. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google may use cookies and similar technologies to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, measure their performance, and prevent fraud. You can learn how Google uses data from sites like this one at Google's partner-sites policy, and manage your ad preferences at Google Ad Settings.

Outbound links

Highlight links take you to YouTube, which operates under Google's privacy policy. We do not embed or host any video ourselves.

Children

Highlight Corner is a general-audience sports site and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new revision date.
